# PRODUCT.md

## Register

product

## Users

Solo professionals and small teams who run AI coding agents (Claude Code, Codex).
They open the dashboard in short, frequent governance sessions, on a large
monitor, usually in daylight: check what skills exist, see what agents changed,
repair links, get out.

## Product Purpose

skill-loom keeps one canonical, git-versioned vault of agent skills and projects
it into every runtime via symlinks. The dashboard is the governance surface for
that vault. It never invents state: the filesystem and git are the truth.

## Brand Personality

Sober, precise, quietly confident. A tool that disappears into the task.
Monospace where the subject is files, paths, hashes; prose where the user reads.

## Anti-references

SaaS hype dashboards, hero metrics, default dark "terminal" aesthetics,
decorative motion, marketing gradients, card grids for everything.

## Strategic Design Principles

- Every write shows its dry-run plan before asking to confirm.
- Read surfaces are dense (tables); reading surfaces are airy (viewer, 65-75ch).
- Nothing leaves the machine; no external assets, no telemetry.
- Familiar product patterns over invention: tabs, tables, breadcrumbs.
